Interpretation

The target is at least 20% growth in GitHub topic-query counts over two years. Unknown future outcomes remain unlabeled. Features use historical prefixes and training uses only outcomes completed before the test year. These are retrospective snapshot backtests: current API counts and topic assignments can revise history. Five domains and correlated years limit inference. Model scores and permutation diagnostics do not establish commercialization, revenue, or investment returns.

Five commercial-technology domains: Computer Systems & Engineering; AI & Applied Mathematics; Quantitative Finance; FinTech & Digital Finance; Computational Quantum & Matter.
